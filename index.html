Privacy Policy for Jarvis

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant application built and operated by Animesh Mishra ("I", "me") for private use. This policy explains what information Jarvis handles when it is used through WhatsApp.

What Jarvis collects

When a message is sent to the Jarvis WhatsApp number, Jarvis receives:

  • the text of the message, and any content in it that the sender chooses to include
  • the sender's WhatsApp phone number and profile name, as provided by WhatsApp
  • message metadata such as timestamps and message IDs

Jarvis does not collect information from any other source through this integration.

How the information is used

Messages are used only to carry out the commands sent to Jarvis and to send a reply through WhatsApp. They are not used for advertising, profiling or resale.

Storage and retention

Incoming messages are held temporarily in a relay queue until Jarvis retrieves them. Messages are removed from the queue once processed, and in any case after a short retention period of no more than 24 hours. Jarvis may keep its own records of commands and results for the operation of the assistant.

Sharing

Information is not sold or shared with third parties, except with service providers needed to run the service (Meta Platforms for WhatsApp messaging, and Cloudflare for hosting the relay). These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask for any data associated with your phone number to be deleted at any time by emailing animesh.pm17@iihmr.in with the subject "Delete my data". I will delete the data and confirm by reply. You can also stop using Jarvis at any time by no longer messaging the number.

Security

Messages are received over encrypted connections, and incoming webhooks are verified before they are accepted. No system is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Jarvis is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
